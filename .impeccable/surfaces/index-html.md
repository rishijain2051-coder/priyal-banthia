## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a wholesale linesheet, the document a D2C fashion label sends a buyer, with Priyal as the label and her eleven live accounts as the styles on the sheet. It refuses the freelancer portfolio arrangement entirely: no hero portrait band, no grid of same-size service cards, no "selected work". It also refuses the fashion-magazine reading of its own source, the cream ground and high-contrast serif, because a real linesheet is an unglamorous trade document printed on bright white, and that honesty is the whole reason the form persuades.

OWN-WORLD: Bright white coated sheet, pure black ink, hairline rules that do the structural work no box or card is allowed to do. One typeface, Archivo variable, worked across its full width and weight range so the hierarchy is a trade document's, set in tabular figures throughout. The only colour on the sheet is a colourway chip per style, taken from the real account, plus one trade red reserved for stamps, ticks and the order marks. Every row is ruled, never enclosed; a card anywhere on this page is a failure of the world.

STORY: A founder scanning on a phone sees a priced-and-coded sheet of eleven brands she has actually run, opens two of them on Instagram, and understands the range without reading a claim. A recruiter finds the provenance block and the line of services. Both end at the order form, where the action is to select styles and send them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone first, 390px. The sheet masthead, flush left, hairline-ruled: PRIYAL BANTHIA at display width, under it SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER and D2C FASHION & LIFESTYLE in small tracked caps, then the trade header strip of tabular figures, 25+ ACCOUNTS WORKED / 11 STYLES ON THIS SHEET / 7 COMPOSITIONS, each separated by a rule. Below the fold line, the first style rows begin, so the sheet is visibly a sheet from the first screen. The primary action is a black ink slab, REQUEST THESE STYLES, sitting in the masthead block.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the sheet is orderable. Each style row carries a tick; ticking it adds the style number and brand to a running order slip pinned at the foot of the sheet, and REQUEST THESE STYLES opens a mail draft already carrying the selected style numbers. Without JavaScript the slip is absent, every row still links out, and the primary action is a plain mailto. Motion is a trade document's: a rule drawing, a stamp landing, a tick inking. Nothing floats and nothing fades in.

FORM: The Linesheet, candidate 1 of my ordered grounded list, served as the pick card and then pinned by the user over both the assigned roll and the bolder hand. Seed key 199bc960.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
