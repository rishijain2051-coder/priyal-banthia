## Direction contract

THESIS: The page re-skins itself eleven times. Her craft is disappearing into other people's brands, so the site performs that instead of describing it: typeface, colour, scale, alignment and mood change completely for each account, and the only constant is her name. It refuses the portfolio arrangement, and it refuses the film metaphor outright — no frame, no timecode, no playhead, no scene slate anywhere, because that idea belongs to another build.

OWN-WORLD: A near-black frame world (#07070A) with bone light and one smoke grey, holding eleven borrowed identities. Three type voices do the shapeshifting: Bodoni Moda for the formal and the jewelled, Big Shoulders Display for the loud, Geist for the clinical and the industrial. The frame is monochrome and never coloured; every colour on the page belongs to an account, which is what makes the switch land. Fixed film grain over everything as material. Nothing is enclosed in a card: each identity is full-bleed, edge to edge.

STORY: I don't have a style, I have twenty-five → every brand you follow has a voice and someone has to be it → watch me become eleven of them → here is what that actually takes → hand me the account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone, 390x844, black. The hook set in two faces at one cap height, so the thesis is visible before a single scroll: "I DON'T HAVE A STYLE." in Bodoni Moda at 900, "I HAVE TWENTY-FIVE." in Big Shoulders. Beneath it a hairline, then PRIYAL BANTHIA / SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER / AHMEDABAD in 11px tracked caps. Her portrait graded cold, low contrast, bled off the right edge behind the type. Bottom left, a breathing hairline as the scroll cue. The hire action is deliberately deferred: the first screen sells the premise.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the switch. Eleven full-bleed identities stacked as sticky scenes, each taking the whole screen with its own face, colour, scale and alignment, cutting rather than fading. Her name is fixed in the corner throughout and never changes; its colour is driven from the live scene's own foreground token by observer, not by mix-blend-mode difference, which was tested and rejected because difference over a mid-tone field cannot be guaranteed to reach AA. Type arrives blur-to-sharp with weight and no bounce. Under reduced motion every identity is simply present and the loader does not exist.

FORM: Brief-pinned, not rolled. The user specified the world in words after rejecting the linesheet, then rejected a reel-shaped direction as too close to an earlier build for a different client. Story order leads with the proof rather than the previously pinned Hero/what/where/accounts/connect, because the brief asks the first screens to stop the visitor. Seed key 199bc960.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
