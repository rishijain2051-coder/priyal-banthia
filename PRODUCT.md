## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS, single file, no build step. User's explicit choice over React+Vite and Astro, so the page can be
drag-and-dropped to Netlify / Vercel / GitHub Pages and handed to anyone later without a toolchain.

## Users

Two confirmed audiences, given equal weight by the user's answer ("Both"):

1. **Brand owners and agency leads** evaluating Priyal for freelance or contract social media work. Mostly Indian D2C,
   jewellery, skincare, fashion, hospitality and F&B. They arrive wanting to know whether she has run accounts like
   theirs, and they decide by looking at the accounts themselves.
2. **Recruiters and hiring managers** for full-time social media / performance marketing roles. She is flagged
   "Open to work" on LinkedIn for Ahmedabad and remote/hybrid. They scan for titles, employers, tools and credentials.

Both audiences land on one page. Neither should have to scroll past the other's content to find their own.

## Product Purpose

A single public page that proves Priyal can run a brand's social presence, and makes starting a conversation with her
trivial. Success is a message to her inbox or a LinkedIn connection. It replaces the PDF deck she currently sends,
which buries the strongest evidence (the real accounts) behind six pages of mission statements.

## Positioning

She reports having worked with 25+ clients. Eleven of those are named, publicly verifiable brand accounts across
seven categories,
and she has since moved up from executing social media into coordinating D2C performance marketing accounts. The
combination a neighbouring freelancer cannot truthfully copy: MICA training, a real multi-category brand roster with
live links, and operational project-management experience on paid D2C accounts rather than organic posting alone.

## Operating Context

Daily content calendars across multiple accounts at once. Content ideation, curation and scheduling for Instagram,
Facebook and LinkedIn. Meta Ads. Keyword, hashtag and link optimisation. Client approval cycles, weekly client calls,
reporting to management, invoice follow-up, and account-health/retention monitoring in her current project-management
role.

  per-account compensation table. It is source material for the role and responsibilities only. No salary, rate,
  band, or per-brand figure is ever published or implied.

  (her own screenshot) and her own LinkedIn audience at 3,186 followers. Every other account is listed in her deck

  portfolio; held in reserve, not published.

## Product Principles

1. **The accounts are the portfolio.** Eleven real handles a visitor can open and judge outrank any claim written
   about them. Link out, never screenshot-as-proxy where a link exists.
2. **Proof before pitch.** The deck opens with a mission statement and reaches the work on page seven. Invert that.
3. **Never invent a number.** An empty, clearly-marked metric slot is worth more than a plausible fabrication, because
   she has to defend every figure in a client call.
4. **Two readers, one page, no detour.** A brand owner and a recruiter each find their own evidence without wading
   through the other's.
5. **She has to be able to maintain it.** One file, plain HTML, no build. Adding a twelfth brand is editing one list.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No user-specific requirement established. Standard applies: WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-reachable controls and
visible focus, full experience without motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the page remains legible and
complete with every enhancement unavailable.
